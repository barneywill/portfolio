HBase Master Initialization Failed After Cluster Restart

📅 June 16, 2022
📖 1 min read
👁️ 837 views

After restarting the HBase cluster, the cluster did not come up normally. The root cause was a master initialization failure, and the master startup log showed the following:

2022-05-26 14:06:15,645 WARN org.apache.hadoop.hbase.master.HMaster: hbase:namespace,,1607716627354.56dafb9f3eadaae9e95d5b05f3142a34. is NOT online; state={56dafb9f3eadaae9e95d5b05f3142a34 state=OPEN, ts=1637906648217, server=hadoop-server1,16020,1637905629938}; ServerCrashProcedures=false. Master startup cannot progress, in holding-pattern until region onlined.

The issue was that the region for the critical hbase:namespace table could not come online. The region details were:

I tried manually assigning the region to bring it online:

hbase(main):034:0> assign '56dafb9f3eadaae9e95d5b05f3142a34'
ERROR: org.apache.hadoop.hbase.PleaseHoldException: Master is initializing

This created a dependency loop: recovering the region required the master to start, but starting the master required this region to already be online.

There were two possible approaches:

  • Back up the namespace directory at /user/hbase/data/hbase/namespace, delete hbase:namespace from hbase:meta, and then restore it. This is relatively difficult and risky.
  • Use the hbck2 tool.

Download link: https://github.com/apache/hbase-operator-tools/tree/master/hbase-hbck2

Run the command:

hbase hbck - j hbase-hbck2-1.1.0.jar assigns 56dafb9f3eadaae9e95d5b05f3142a34

After running it, hbase:namespace recovered and the master started successfully.
