IntelliJ IDEA Fails to Start on macOS 12

📅 September 08, 2021
📖 1 min read
👁️ 1892 views

For the m1 version of mac, using macOS 11 Big Sur can start idea normally, but macOS 12 Monterey fails to start, prompting

Idea quit unexpectedly, click "Reopen" to open the application again
idea quit unexpectedly, Click Reopen to open the application again.

Revise

~/Library/Application\ Support/JetBrains/IdeaIC2021.2/idea.vmoptions

Add two lines

-XX:+TieredCompilation
-XX:TieredStopAtLevel=1

problem solving
