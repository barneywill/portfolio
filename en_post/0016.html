Impala ORC Query Support

📅 May 20, 2022
📖 1 min read
👁️ 550 views

Impala supports ORC format query starting from version 3.1, but it is experimental

To enable the method, add --enable_ORC_scanner=true in the Impala configuration of CDH

Then restart the Impala cluster.

Official documentation:
https://docs.cloudera.com/documentation/enterprise/latest/topics/impala_orc.html
