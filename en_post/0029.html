Hadoop 4 - HDFS Trash

📅 April 06, 2022
📖 1 min read
👁️ 583 views

Configuration

Configuration 1

fs.trash.interval=1440

Deleted files will go into the Recycle Bin and will be kept for 1440 minutes by default before being completely deleted.

Configuration 2

fs.trash.checkpoint.interval=60

Recycle Bin checkpoint mechanism: Files entering the Recycle Bin first enter the Current directory, and then are moved to a timestamp directory regularly, with a regular interval of 60 minutes.

process

1 delete command

hdfs dfs rm /tmp/test

2 Enter the recycle bin

hdfs://${nn}/user/${user_name}/.Trash/Current/tmp/test

3. Regular inspection

hdfs://${nn}/user/${user_name}/.Trash/Current/tmp/test

move to

hdfs://${nn}/user/${user_name}/.Trash/${yyMMddHH0000}/tmp/test

4 Regular inspection (after 24 hours)

Delete completely

Remark

Between step 2 and step 3, the data in the recycle bin can be restored at any time by moving it to its original location.

Special attention should be paid to the fact that in step 2, File Not Exists may be prompted during the operation. Don't panic at this time. The file is not lost. In fact, step 3 has been performed and the file has been moved to another directory.
