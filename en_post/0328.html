Ps：

  • Quartz has two deployment methods:Standaloneas well asdistributed(now also supports the new TerracottaJobStore),StandaloneDue to the RAMJobStore used, there is no way to automatically resume previously interrupted tasks after restarting, so based onStandaloneThe encapsulation mainly consists of two points, one is scheduler distributed control, and the other is automatic task recovery after scheduler restarts. These two points aredistributedhas been implemented in , so based onStandaloneThe encapsulation seems unnecessary;
  • Both Azkaban and zeus provide their own execution nodes (executor server in Azkaban and worker in zeus) to execute tasks, and both execute tasks by starting local processes. This will cause two problems:
    • The scheduling node scheduler needs to maintain a list of available execution node executors (zeus uses long connections, which does a better job), and resumes tasks when the execution node executor is abnormal, and it is easy to cause some problems due to untimely status synchronization. This is a relatively huge overhead, and Oozie does not need this at all;
    • Start a local process to execute a task. Although the resources of the task process can be flexibly controlled, there are tasksRepeatedly execute at the same timerisk, unless the task itself is guaranteed not to be executed repeatedly at the same time through zk;

  • Compared with Azkaban, zeus does not do well in one thing, it is all-in-one, that is, the master and slave are deployed together. There are two problems, one is the waste of resources, and the other is the mutual influence between the master and the slave;

The abstractions of all scheduling frameworks are the same or similar, so in theory the scheduling framework A can beTask, workflow, and scheduling definitions Translate For scheduling framework BTask, workflow, and scheduling definitions, to realize switching of scheduling framework;

 

An analogy is that scheduling frame A is a Chinese worker, scheduling frame B is a Japanese worker, and the Chinese worker is sick. Now it is necessary to add a translator C to tell the Japanese worker the content and time of the Chinese worker's work, and at the same time, continuously synchronize the progress of the Japanese worker's work to the Chinese worker. All changes are transparent to the leadership;
