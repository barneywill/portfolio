Spark tasks are unevenly distributed among executors

📅 December 20, 2018
📖 1 min read
👁️ 2246 views

I recently submitted a Spark application and found that the execution was very slow. After clicking on the Spark web ui, I found that it was stuck on a stage of a job. This stage has 100,000 tasks, but most of the tasks are assigned to two executors, and the other executors are very idle. What happened?

Looking at the Spark task allocation logic, we found that there is a feature of data locality, which is data locality. For details, see https://www.cnblogs.com/barneywill/p/10152497.html
That is, tasks will be allocated according to the priority of the locality level. The priorities of data locality are: PROCESS_LOCAL, NODE_LOCAL, NO_PREF, RACK_LOCAL, ANY, and there is a delay between the priorities.

spark.locality.wait

After further checking the current cluster deployment structure, we found that DataNode and nodemanager were not deployed together. Therefore, when Spark allocates NODE_LOCAL type tasks, only two executors meet the conditions, so most tasks are allocated to these two executors. By setting

spark.locality.wait=0

solve problems;

ps: Storage and computing (i.e. DataNode and nodemanager) should either be deployed completely separately or completely together. Do not deploy some separately and some together. If this must be done, do not enable the data localization feature;
