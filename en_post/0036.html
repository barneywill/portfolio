Try the following:

  • Map the host’s /etc/hosts into the container
  • Add --add-host=NameNode1:192.168.0.1
  • Add --net=host

The problem remains. According to the official documentation, modify the /etc/hosts file and change

192.168.0.1 namenode1

Change to (add a dot at the end)

192.168.0.1 namenode1 namenode1.
