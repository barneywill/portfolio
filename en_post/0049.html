Tonghuashun Keyboard Shortcuts

📅 August 24, 2020
📖 1 min read
👁️ 4179 views

Commonly used shortcut keys

Esc Back
Enter Switch list / intraday chart / candlestick chart

# Market
F3 Shanghai Composite
F4 Shenzhen Component

F6 Watchlist

# Individual stocks
F1 Trade details
F2 Price-volume distribution
F5 Switch intraday chart / candlestick chart
F7 Full stock overview
F8 Analysis period
F9 Stock diagnosis
F10 Company information
F11 Basic information

Insert Add to watchlist
Delete Remove from watchlist

# Split screen
Ctrl+4 4 stocks in columns
Ctrl+9 9 stocks in columns
Ctrl+6 16 stocks in columns

# Multiple periods
Ctrl+F8 Multi-period chart

# Common features
Ctrl+D Market comparison
Ctrl+F Formula editor
Ctrl+T Tick trend
Ctrl+Z Zoom right-side cell
Ctrl+R View sectors
Ctrl+F6 Large font
Ctrl+W Full screen
Ctrl+X Draw lines

# Switch markets
1+Enter Shanghai A-shares
3+Enter Shenzhen A-shares
9+Enter Hong Kong stocks
002+Enter SME Board
300+Enter ChiNext
688+Enter STAR Market
666+Enter Shanghai/Shenzhen indexes
800+Enter Foreign exchange
888+Enter Stock index futures
999+Enter Major indexes

90+Enter Multi-window market view
91+Enter Major buy/sell orders
94+Enter Sector hotspots

# Rankings: 6 gainers ranking, 8 comprehensive ranking
60/80+Enter Shanghai/Shenzhen comprehensive ranking
61/81+Enter Shanghai A-share comprehensive ranking
63/83+Enter Shenzhen A-share comprehensive ranking
602/802+Enter SME Board comprehensive ranking
603/803+Enter ChiNext comprehensive ranking

# Candlestick chart
Alt+N N-chart combination
31+Enter 1 minute
32+Enter 5 minutes
33+Enter 15 minutes
34+Enter 30 minutes
35+Enter 60 minutes
36+Enter Day
37+Enter Week
38+Enter Month
39+Enter Quarter
310+Enter Year
Down Zoom out
Up Zoom in
Ctrl+Right/Left Move cursor quickly
Tab Show/hide moving averages
