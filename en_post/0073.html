After checking mesos, it was found that mesos was abnormal. The final reason was that the data between ZooKeeper nodes was out of sync. Mesos maintains master leader information on ZooKeeper.

[zk: 192.168.0.1:2181(CONNECTED) 1] ls /mesos

[json.info_0000000114, log_replicas, json.info_0000000115]

This data is out of sync and causes mesos to not be able to find the master, which in turn causes marathon exceptions. Stop the problematic ZooKeeper node, clear the data directory and restart it. After the data is consistent, the problem is solved;
