gz file related operations

📅 March 19, 2019
📖 1 min read
👁️ 1059 views

gz files do not need to be decompressed to perform related operations

$ zcat test.log.gz
$ zmore test.log.gz
$ zless test.log.gz
$ zgrep '1.2.3.4' test.log.gz
$ egrep 'regex' test.log.gz
