The earlier commands had started multiple procedures that were all trying to operate on the same region. They were blocked behind the first procedure because that procedure still held the lock.

hbase hbck -j hbase-operator-tools-1.1.0/hbase-hbck2/hbase-hbck2-1.1.0.jar bypass -o -r $PROCEDURE_PID

Using hbck2 to bypass those procedures resolved the issue.
