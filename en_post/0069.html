1 Mac

1 Text editing (emacs style)

Cursor movement

Ctrl+A (Ahead line start)

Ctrl+E (End end of line)

Ctrl+P (Previous previous line/previous command in the command line)

Ctrl+N (Next next line/next command in the command line)

Ctrl+B (Backward moves one character to the left)

Ctrl+F (Forward moves one character to the right)

Ctrl+M (Enter)

Opt+Left (move one word to the left)

Opt+Right (move one word to the right)

Cmd+Left (beginning of line)

Cmd+Right (end of line)

Cmd+Up (beginning of file)

Cmd+Down (end of file)

delete

Ctrl+H (Backspace to delete to the left)

Ctrl+D (Delete delete to the right)

Ctrl+W (Delete one word to the left)

Ctrl+K (delete right to end of line)

Ctrl+U (Delete to the left to the beginning of the line)

Ctrl+T (swap the two characters before and after the cursor)

Ctrl+Y (Restore)

selected

Shift+Left (select one character to the left)

Shift+Right (select one character to the right)

Shift+Opt+Left (select a word to the left)

Shift+Opt+Right (select a word to the right)

Shift+Cmd+Left (select left to the beginning of the line)

Shift+Cmd+Right (select to the right to the end of the line)
