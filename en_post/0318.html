Text replacement of commonly used Linux commands

1 vi

vi test_file

:%s/h/h1/g

Note: Full text replacement, replace h with h1

:1,4s/h/h1/g

Note: Replace h in lines 1 to 4 with h1

:%s/\n/,/g

Note: Replace the newline character with,

2 sed

sed - e 's/h/h1/g' test_file

Note: Full text replacement, replace h with h1

sed - e '1,4s/h/h1/g' test_file

Note: Replace h in lines 1 to 4 with h1

sed ':a;N;$!ba;s/\n/,/g' test_file

Note: Replace the newline character with,

sed 'nd' test_file

Comment: Delete the nth line

sed - e '1s/h/h1/g;4s/h/h1/g' test_file

Note: Multiple replacements at the same time, separated by ;

 

If you want to modify the file directly, add the -i parameter;

3 direct replacement

$ echo ${var//a/b}
