beeline connects to hiveserver2 and reports an error impersonate

📅 March 07, 2019
📖 1 min read
👁️ 681 views

beeline reports error when connecting to hiveserver2

Error: Could not open client transport with JDBC Uri: jdbc:hive2://localhost:10000: Failed to open new session: java.lang.RuntimeException: org.apache.hadoop.ipc.RemoteException(org.apache.hadoop.security.authorize.AuthorizationException): User: test is not allowed to impersonate anonymous (state=08S01,code=0)

Solution

core-site.xml

     <property>

         <name>hadoop.proxyuser.test.groups</name>

         <value>*</value>

     </property>

     <property>

         <name>hadoop.proxyuser.test.hosts</name>

         <value>*</value>

     </property>

This is the test user. Other users can adjust it themselves, that is, execute the user name of beeline. After the modification is completed, restart HDFS and YARN.
