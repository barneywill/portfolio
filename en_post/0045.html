HBase HMaster Startup Error

📅 May 13, 2021
📖 1 min read
👁️ 464 views

Phenomenon

HBase hmaster startup error

master.HMaster: Failed to become active master java.lang.IllegalStateException: The procedure WAL relies on the ability to hsync for proper operation during component failures, but the underlying filesystem does not support doing so. Please check the config value of 'hbase.procedure.store.wal.use.hsync' to set the desired level of robustness and ensure the config value of 'hbase.wal.dir' points to a FileSystem mount that can provide it.

environment

  • hbase1.6
  • hadoop2.9

Two methods:

1) Not recommended

<property>
  <name>hbase.unsafe.stream.capability.enforce</name>
  <value>false</value>
</property>

2) Change hadoop dependencies

HBase is pre-compiled and the hadoop version that the compilation depends on is 2.8.5. The actual hadoop used is 2.9.2. Replace all $HBASE_HOME/lib/hadoop-*-2.8.5.jar with hadoop-*-2.9.2.jar. The required new version jar package is in this directory: $HADOOP_HOME/share/hadoop/

After the operation, just restart HBase
