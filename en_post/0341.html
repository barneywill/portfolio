To sum up the above three points, why ClassNotFoundException is still reported? Two background knowledge is needed here:

One is the Spark submit process, see for details https://www.cnblogs.com/barneywill/p/9820684.html

One is Java's classloader, see for details https://www.cnblogs.com/barneywill/p/10111633.html

 

Three problem playback process

1 spark-submit
2 spark-class
3 org.apache.spark.launcher.Main
4 org.apache.spark.deploy.SparkSubmit
4.1 Initialize MutableURLClassLoader, add --jars and application jar as classpath to MutableURLClassLoader (note that zkclient-0.3.jar is here at this time), then load the application class, that is, AppClass, through MutableURLClassLoader, and then call main by reflection
4.2 Indirectly calling Kafka.utils.ZKGroupDirs in AppClass.main (Kafka jar is located in Spark.driver.extraClassPath), ZKGroupDirs is loaded by the parent classloader of MutableURLClassLoader, which is AppClassLoader, and Kafka.utils.ZKGroupDirs depends on org.I 0Itec.zkclient.exception.ZkNoNodeException is also loaded by AppClassLoader, not by MutableURLClassLoader, so it is reported that it cannot be loaded, although it seems that zkclient-0.3.jar is indeed in the classpath;

To solve the problem, you need to add zkclient-0.3.jar to the classpath of AppClassLoader (i.e. Spark.driver.extraClassPath)
