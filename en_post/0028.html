Bluetooth Failure on Linux

📅 May 13, 2022
📖 1 min read
👁️ 427 views

Bluetooth sometimes fails on the Linux desktop. At this time, all Bluetooth devices cannot connect. The problem cannot be solved by restarting or turning Bluetooth on and off, and the device cannot be restored by deleting and re-adding it.
Bluetooth command line

bluetoothctl

View status

rfkill list

Found that Bluetooth is in blocked state

rfkill unblock all

Bluetooth recovery
