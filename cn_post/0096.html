hive查询报错NoViableAltException

📅 July 30, 2019
📖 1 min read
👁️ 6387 views

Hive或spark中执行sql字符常量包含;时会报错，比如

select instr('abc;abc', ';');

报错

NoViableAltException(-1@[147:1: selectExpression : ( expression | tableAllColumns );])

需要将;改为ascii

select instr('abc\073abc', '\073');
